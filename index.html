Method

How the numbers are worked out

  • ✓Void, draft and uncollectible invoices are excluded from billed, outstanding and the chase list.
  • ✓Outstanding uses amount remaining, so a part-paid invoice only counts what's still owed.
  • ✓Everything is computed as of the selected date: invoices created later are ignored, and an invoice paid after that date counts as outstanding on it.
  • ✓Collected means amount paid, dated by the paid-at date. Weeks run Monday to Sunday; the current week runs to the as-of date.
  • ✓Days overdue counts from the due date (or the invoice date for auto-charged invoices with no due date). Invoices 1 to 6 days late are listed as in grace with no follow-up drafted.
  • ✓Column names are matched loosely, so "Amount Due", "amount_due" and "Total" all work. Revenue stream comes from a stream metadata column if present, otherwise it's inferred from the description.
